# Workspace (workspace.html)

Mode: Operate. Reader opens, reads, annotates a PDF and asks VENMA about selected passages. Must keep every existing function, the non-destructive PDF rule, honest model labels, and an optional, dismissable pet.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The workspace is a reading desk. Annotations are sticky flags on the page edge and VENMA lives on the desk with you, alive. Refuses the grey reader-with-sidebar.

OWN-WORLD: Lavender desk-mat ground, navy ink bar and type, white paper page, highlighter yellow, pink and mint as the only annotation hues, VENMA lavender with amber eyes. Rounded Nunito throughout, soft 10–16px corners, neutral offset shadows like paper on a mat. Colour only where it has a job.

STORY: Drop a PDF on the desk, read, highlight; each mark grows a flag on the page edge. Select text and VENMA perks up, explains in the drawer with its model named, and the answer can be kept as a flag.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Navy bar across the top: VENMA mark, document name, page, zoom, undo/redo, Export. Lavender mat below with the white page centred and flags off its right edge. Floating VENMA bottom-right on the mat; the white answer drawer opens above it on the right.

FORM: Study Desk, position 1 of 7 (picked by the user over the rolled Gallery Guide). seed:8c2d1d63d47c4baf

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

## Signature interaction

VENMA is a layered vector cat: breathes, blinks at random intervals, twitches an ear, sways its tail, pupils follow the pointer; moods change pose (curious tilt and perked ears on selection, thinking dots while a model works, happy squint on success, flattened ears on error). Reduced motion holds a still pose.
